Standards and quality

Certified welders, documented procedures

Weld quality is not only checked at the end. It is ensured by qualified welders, the right welding procedure and documented inspection.

ISO 9606-1Qualification testing of welders – fusion welding of steels

Our welders are certified to EN ISO 9606-1 for the processes, positions and material groups they use in their work.

WPS · WPQREN ISO 15609-1 · EN ISO 15614-1

We prepare welding procedure specifications (WPS) for every project. Where a qualified procedure is required, we carry out procedure qualification (WPQR) to EN ISO 15614-1.

Inspection and documentationVisual inspection · NDT · reports

Welds are inspected during fabrication and before handover. The type of non-destructive testing and the scope of documentation follow the project requirements.

Welder certificates, WPS documents and inspection reports are provided as required by the project. Process numbers are given according to ISO 4063.

FAQ

Questions from clients

Are your welders certified?

Yes, our welders are certified to EN ISO 9606-1. We prepare a welding procedure specification (WPS) for every project and carry out procedure qualification (WPQR to EN ISO 15614-1) when the project requires it.

Which materials do you weld?

Carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium. The process is selected according to the material, wall thickness, weld position and documentation requirements.

Do you offer non-destructive testing?

Yes. The type of inspection is defined by the project requirements, and results are documented in reports.

Do you work on site and abroad?

Yes. We work in our own workshop or at the client's site, in Slovenia and abroad.

Do you also produce series?

We produce single pieces, small batches and series production.

Send us your drawings or a project description.

We prepare a quotation based on scope, materials, required standards and delivery date.
