About the company

ARC METAL is a Slovenian company for industrial welding and installation. We build on expertise, continuous training and a responsible attitude towards our clients.

We believe that knowledge, experience and continuous development are the foundation of well-executed projects. Since the company was founded, we have built it on a professional approach, responsibility and long-term thinking.

We place particular emphasis on continuous education and training of our staff, so that we keep up with new technologies, welding processes and modern industrial standards. We work with accredited training and certification bodies, through which we gain new knowledge, qualifications and internationally recognised certificates.

Investing in people means investing in the quality of our work.

Standards and quality

How we ensure weld quality

ISO 9606-1Qualification testing of welders – fusion welding of steels

Our welders are certified to EN ISO 9606-1 for the processes, positions and material groups they use in their work.

WPS · WPQREN ISO 15609-1 · EN ISO 15614-1

We prepare welding procedure specifications (WPS) for every project. Where a qualified procedure is required, we carry out procedure qualification (WPQR) to EN ISO 15614-1.

Inspection and documentationVisual inspection · NDT · reports

Welds are inspected during fabrication and before handover. The type of non-destructive testing and the scope of documentation follow the project requirements.

Welder certificates, WPS documents and inspection reports are provided as required by the project. Process numbers are given according to ISO 4063.
